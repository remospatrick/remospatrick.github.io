    1. Who We Are

    Artisan Solutions is a Yardi consulting firm based in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates. We provide helpdesk support, report customization, and data migration services to enterprise real estate and property management firms in Ireland, the United States, and Saudi Arabia.

    For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, Artisan Solutions is the data controller responsible for your personal information.

    Contact: info@artisanitsolutions.com

    2. Information We Collect

    We collect information only when you voluntarily provide it through our website contact form. This may include:

    • First and last name
    • Work email address
    • Company name
    • Location / market (Ireland, USA, or Saudi Arabia)
    • Service of interest
    • Any information you choose to include in your message

    We also collect limited data automatically when you visit our website through Google Analytics 4. This may include:

    • Pages visited and time spent on each page
    • General geographic location (country/city level only)
    • Device type, browser, and operating system
    • How you arrived at the website (direct, search, referral)
    • Interactions with buttons and the contact form (anonymized)

    This data is anonymized and aggregated. We cannot identify individual visitors from it. IP addresses are anonymized before storage.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information you submit solely to:

    • Respond to your inquiry or consultation request
    • Assess whether our services are a good fit for your organization
    • Follow up on previous conversations at your request

    We will never use your information to send unsolicited marketing emails, sell your data to third parties, or share your details with any external party without your explicit consent.

    4. Third-Party Services & Processors

    Our contact form is processed by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form handling service. When you submit the contact form, your data is transmitted to Formspree's servers before being forwarded to us by email.

    Formspree acts as a data processor on our behalf. Their privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

    We also use Google Analytics 4, operated by Google LLC. Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data through cookies to help us understand how visitors use our website. Google may transfer this data to servers in the United States.

    Google Analytics is configured on this website with IP anonymization enabled. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising or remarketing purposes.

    Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

    5. Legal Basis for Processing

    We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

    • Consent: You voluntarily submit your information through our contact form.
    • Legitimate interests: We have a legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries directed to us.

    For visitors from the European Union (including Ireland), we process your data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For visitors from the United States, we comply with applicable state privacy laws. For visitors from Saudi Arabia, we comply with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

    6. Data Retention

    We retain contact form submissions and related correspondence for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected, or as required by applicable law. In practice, this means we keep inquiry emails for up to 24 months unless an active client relationship is established, in which case retention follows our client data policies.

    7. Your Rights

    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data.
    • Objection: Object to our processing of your data for certain purposes.
    • Portability: Request your data in a portable format (where applicable).

    To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@artisanitsolutions.com. We will respond within 30 days.

    8. Data Security

    We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Our website is served over HTTPS and access to submitted inquiry data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

    No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we take all reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    9. Cookies

    Our website uses cookies placed by Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymized usage data. The cookies used are:

    • _ga — distinguishes unique visitors. Expires after 2 years.
    • _ga_XXXXXXX — maintains session state. Expires after 2 years.

    These are analytics cookies only. We do not use advertising cookies, remarketing cookies, or any cookies that track you across other websites.

    You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. You can also opt out of Google Analytics entirely using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

    We do not display a cookie consent banner because we use only anonymized analytics cookies with IP masking enabled, and we do not use cookies for advertising or profiling purposes. If you are visiting from the EU and have concerns, please contact us directly.

    10. Children's Privacy

    Our services are intended for business professionals only. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our website, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

    11. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any revisions. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
