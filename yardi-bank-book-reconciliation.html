How Yardi Bank Book Transforms Property Finance Reconciliation

For many property finance teams, bank reconciliation is the single biggest bottleneck in month-end close. Yardi's Bank Book and Automatic Bank Reconciliation are built to change that fundamentally.

Artisan Solutions 6 min read Yardi Consulting

Ask a property management finance team what takes the most time at month-end and bank reconciliation is almost always in the top two. Across a portfolio of even modest size, reconciling dozens of bank accounts manually against the general ledger, identifying unmatched items, chasing timing differences, and producing a clean close sign-off can consume days that the team simply does not have to spare. The frustration is compounded by the fact that in many cases, Yardi already contains the tools to automate the majority of this work. They are included in the core Voyager license. They just have not been set up.

Yardi's Bank Book and Automatic Bank Reconciliation module represent one of the most operationally impactful capabilities available to property finance teams running on Voyager. When configured correctly, the system auto-clears 60 to 80 percent of transactions automatically, reducing per-account reconciliation time from hours to minutes. Here is what the module does, how it works, and what teams that have adopted it consistently report on the other side.

01
What It Is

Yardi Bank Book: one view of every cash position across the portfolio

Yardi Bank Book is the module within Voyager that maintains a running record of all cash activity for every bank account linked to the portfolio. It sits between the general ledger and the bank statement, giving finance teams a structured, real-time view of cleared transactions, outstanding items, and reconciling differences across every account simultaneously. Rather than working through accounts one at a time using a combination of Voyager screens and external spreadsheets, the team works within a single, organized environment where everything needed for the reconciliation is already in one place.

The three-column structure at the heart of Yardi's bank reconciliation is what makes this work clearly: the GL balance column shows the total of all cash accounts linked to each bank code; the bank information column reflects the ending balance from the bank statement plus any outstanding deposits or payments; and the reconciled balance column shows where the two agree. When they do not agree, the differences are visible immediately, categorized, and traceable back to the specific transactions causing them.

02
The Automation Layer

Automatic Bank Reconciliation: clearing 60-80 percent without manual effort

Yardi's Automatic Bank Reconciliation, known as ABR, takes the core Bank Book capability and adds an automated matching layer. The module imports a bank statement file in MT940 format for most UAE and GCC banks, or BAI2 for US banks, and automatically matches those transactions against the Voyager ledger. Transactions that match on amount, date, and reference are cleared automatically without any manual intervention. Those that do not match are flagged for review with the specific discrepancy highlighted.

The result is that 60 to 80 percent of a typical month's transactions are cleared before the finance team has touched anything. The team's time is directed entirely toward the exceptions: timing differences, bank fees not yet in the ledger, items in transit, and genuine discrepancies that require investigation. This is precisely where skilled finance professionals should be spending their time, not manually matching transactions that a system can match automatically and accurately.

ABR is included in the core Voyager license with the GL Posting Utilities plug-in (version 7.2 or above) at no additional cost. The main investment is in the setup and configuration, which is a one-time activity that continues to deliver value with every reconciliation run that follows.

Most Voyager clients already have access to Automatic Bank Reconciliation but are not using it. When configured well, it clears the majority of transactions automatically and redirects the finance team's time to the work that genuinely requires human judgment.

03
Fraud Detection

A reconciliation process that also protects against financial risk

Regular, timely bank reconciliation is one of the most effective controls against financial fraud in property management. Duplicate check cashing, unauthorized transactions, and payment diversions are all significantly easier to detect when the reconciliation is done frequently and thoroughly. Banks typically allow a window of around three months to report and receive refunds for fraudulent transactions. Organizations that reconcile monthly and investigate exceptions promptly are well within that window. Those that reconcile quarterly, or treat the reconciliation as a formality rather than a control, often discover problems too late to recover the funds.

Yardi Bank Book makes frequent reconciliation practical rather than burdensome. When the automation handles the matching, the team can run a reconciliation every two weeks rather than once a month, without doubling the time commitment. The control becomes stronger, and the exposure window for undetected fraud narrows significantly.

04
Voyager 8 and the Period Close Dashboard

Bank rec as part of a fully managed month-end close

Voyager 8 introduced the Period Close Dashboard, which brings the full month-end close workflow into a single, centralized view: task assignments, due dates, dependencies, and completion status for every close step, including bank reconciliation. Controllers see in real time where the close stands across the entire portfolio, which accounts are cleared, which are in progress, and which are waiting on action. The reconciliation no longer exists in isolation as a separate activity that someone is managing in a spreadsheet on the side.

Combined with Voyager 8's improvements to journal entry processing, including large journals without timeout issues and copy-paste from Excel, and automatic report generation at each milestone, the Period Close Dashboard represents a meaningful step forward in how efficiently a property finance team can close the books each month. Bank reconciliation is one component of that close, and Yardi has invested in making it both faster and more visible to everyone who needs to see where it stands.

05
What Is Coming

AI-powered reconciliation with Yardi Virtuoso

Yardi Virtuoso, launched at YASC 2025, extends the automation layer beyond ABR into AI-powered financial workflows. Early adopters of Virtuoso's financial agent capabilities have reported month-end reconciliation and reporting time dropping from over 20 hours to under 5 hours per property. While Virtuoso adoption is still in its early stages, the direction is clear: Yardi is building toward a close process where AI handles not just transaction matching but also exception investigation, journal entry suggestions, and report generation, with the finance team reviewing and approving rather than building from scratch.

For organizations that set up ABR today and build clean, consistent reconciliation habits within Yardi, the path to adopting Virtuoso's more advanced capabilities is shorter and smoother. The data quality and process discipline that makes ABR work well is the same foundation that makes AI-powered financial workflows trustworthy. Getting there starts with the tools already available in the current license.

Getting started

What a well-configured Yardi bank reconciliation looks like in practice

The following outcomes define what a properly configured Bank Book and ABR setup delivers for a property finance team. Each one is achievable within the current Voyager license for most organizations, with the right configuration in place.

  • MT940 or BAI2 bank statement files imported automatically, with 60-80 percent of transactions matched and cleared without manual intervention
  • Unmatched items flagged with specific discrepancy details, so the team investigates only what genuinely requires attention
  • All bank accounts across the portfolio visible in a single Bank Book view, with reconciliation status current at all times
  • Month-end bank rec completed as part of the Voyager 8 Period Close Dashboard workflow, with completion status visible to controllers in real time
  • Reconciliation frequency increased from monthly to bi-weekly without a proportional increase in team workload
  • Audit-ready reconciliation records maintained within Yardi, with a complete trail of cleared items, exceptions, and adjustments
  • Foundation in place to adopt Yardi Virtuoso's AI-powered financial close capabilities as they become available
